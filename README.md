# Henry's Private Vault

attached is design mockup image . want to create e-shop for elite perfumes with best price available for customers. (since we have good procurement network) domain and site brand should be built around ceo and founder Henry, young, smart, educated, healthy, good looking, into beautitions, health, outfit and finance,  domain would be henryperfumes.eu and *.com . we dont want another standard "luxury eshop" we want to be non-ordinary, having wow shopping experience both in design and speed.
Therefore we will build it on full stack sveltekit and need fast prototype from you, therefore, please analyse mockup image, prompt below and 
Build a high-fidelity interactive prototype of henryperfumes.eu — a private fragrance curator’s e-shop design proposal (no backend no auth just visible few page demo).

Brand & positioning

- Domain: henryperfumes.eu

- One man (Henry, 28, Prague) buys elite perfumes direct and sells at honest prices.

- Tagline: “Bought direct. Priced honest. Chosen by one man.”

- Design system: Dark Cinematic Noir — pure black (#000000 / #0B0B0D), bone (#EDE7DB), gold (#C9A961). Serif display (Canela / GT Sectra style), clean geometric sans for UI.

- Atmosphere: gallery / private vault, not luxury marketplace. Deep negative space, one product in the spotlight at a time, slow cinematic reveals, no urgency, no “bestsellers”, no chat widgets.

Key pages to generate (desktop + mobile):

1. Homepage / Viewing Room

   - Full-bleed black background with soft radial gold spotlight.

   - Center: large geometric crystal perfume bottle (faceted, gold collar) under dramatic top light.

   - Left: circular portrait of Henry (young man, black turtleneck, half-face in shadow, serious expression).

   - Headline: “Your curator tonight.” or “Tonight’s selection.”

   - Subtle gold line accents, “BOUGHT DIRECT.” label, price comparison (retail struck through).

   - Minimal top nav: henry. logo left, sparse links, cart icon.

2. Vault (catalog) — restrained grid of VaultCards with gold edge on hover only.

3. Product Ceremony page (/p/[slug]) — five-act scroll experience (hero image → notes → Henry’s note → provenance → acquire).

4. Simple /henry page with portrait and short bio.

5. Checkout (full-screen, silent, minimal).

Interaction & motion

- Fade transitions only (no slides, bounces, springs).

- Soft gold cursor on interactive elements.

- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

- Hover on product cards: only a thin gold border appears.

Voice (strict)

- First person (Henry). Never “we”.

- Banned words: luxurious, luxury, amazing, must-have, trending, bestseller, discover, indulge, exclusive, elevate…

- Preferred: consider, worth, underrated, acquire, note, batch, house, sillage.

- CTAs: “ACQUIRE”, “Add to list”.

- Microcopy examples: “You haven’t chosen anything yet.” / “Gone. Next batch arrives [month].” / “Confirmed. Shipping tomorrow. — H”

Do not include: chatbots, urgency banners, related products, reviews on PDP, cookie walls larger than a strip, social share buttons, emoji.

Make the homepage match the attached dark cinematic mockup as closely as possible (spotlight bottle, Henry portrait left, gold typography, pure black void).

Use high-quality placeholder perfume photography and a realistic young male portrait in the described style.
